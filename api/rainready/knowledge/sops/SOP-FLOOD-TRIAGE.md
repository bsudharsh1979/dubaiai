> DEMONSTRATION PROCEDURE written for the RainReady UAE prototype. Not an official government policy or an agency SOP.

## S1 Labels and what they mean
Keep the six labels separate at all times: observed water (a sensor or scene shows water now/at acquisition), susceptibility (static terrain/drainage context), forecast risk (a scenario model projects accumulation), anomaly (telemetry departs from expectation), suspected incident (correlated evidence but unconfirmed), confirmed incident (authorized field/utility confirmation). Never promote a label without new evidence.

## S2 Triage bands
Rainfall triage uses ordinal bands from the scenario twin relative to each asset's synthetic threshold: low (<25% of threshold), moderate (25–60%), high (60–100%), severe (≥100%). Bands are uncalibrated; do not present them as probabilities. A high or severe band with insufficient or weak evidence quality is routed to urgent human review rather than downgraded or auto-dispatched.

## S3 Minimum evidence for a proposal
Before a response proposal is drafted the coordinator must hold at least two independent sources among: rain gauge (current), water-level sensor (current), authorized forecast (issued within its freshness window), satellite candidate water (acquisition within context window, clearly dated), field report (reviewed). Repeated reports from a single source count once.

## S4 Underpass actions
Candidate actions in order of preference: 1) inspection of the underpass and its drain inlets, 2) mobile pump deployment where installed capacity is below modelled inflow, 3) access-control recommendation to the transport controller when synthetic depth is projected to exceed the asset threshold within +60 min. Access control is a recommendation to the transport authority; it is never executed by the system.

## S5 Reassessment
Every approved package schedules a reassessment after the shortest action horizon. Closure requires a new observation showing conditions below threshold and an authorized incident commander's decision. Agents may not self-certify safety.
