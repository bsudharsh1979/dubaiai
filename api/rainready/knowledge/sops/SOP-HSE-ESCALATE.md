> DEMONSTRATION PROCEDURE for the RainReady UAE prototype. Not an official HSE standard.

## S1 Scope
This procedure covers potential exposure of a model electrical installation, an occupied worksite or an emergency access route to observed or forecast water. It does not attempt to predict every HSE incident.

## S2 Exposure classes
Electrical: water within the asset buffer or synthetic depth at or above the asset threshold → recommend an electrical-safety review by the asset owner. Occupied worksite: recommend site supervisor notification and a stop-work consideration. Emergency access: recommend the transport controller assesses alternative candidate routes; routes remain "candidate — requires verification".

## S3 Authority
The HSE officer persona recommends review. It cannot issue evacuation orders or electrical switching instructions. Those decisions sit with the human incident commander and the asset owner; the system has no control endpoint for them.

## S4 Reports of injury or death
Any report mentioning injury, casualties or fatalities is preserved and escalated to the incident commander for review immediately, regardless of any content-classifier flag. Public text must not include unverified casualty claims.
