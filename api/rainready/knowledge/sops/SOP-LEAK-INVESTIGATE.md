> DEMONSTRATION PROCEDURE for the RainReady UAE prototype. Not a utility's official procedure.

## S1 Anomaly, not incident
A flow/pressure residual anomaly is labelled *anomaly* until competing explanations are ruled out and labelled *suspected incident* when the joint signature (sustained flow above expectation with pressure below expectation on the same segment) persists with no plausible operational explanation. *Confirmed incident* requires a utility duty engineer's or field crew's confirmation.

## S2 Competing explanations to check
Planned operations (valve exercises, flushing, reservoir transfers), maintenance windows, demand changes (events, holidays, temperature), rainfall-driven inflow or surface water that could explain wet ground, and sensor health (meter drift, communications gaps). Absent telemetry yields *insufficient evidence*, never a negative finding.

## S3 Location
Record the suspected location as an uncertain network segment or area polygon. A dark satellite patch or a wet-ground photograph near a main does not confirm a burst; it is a correlation to be checked.

## S4 Investigation package
Propose an inspection of the candidate segment (acoustic/visual), a request for the utility's SCADA confirmation and, where pressure loss could affect supply to critical assets, an HSE review. The system has no actuator access; valve operations are out of scope.
